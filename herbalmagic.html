Packaging Design & Creative Direction

Led the creative direction and packaging design for new product launches, including the complete conceptualization of the Kickstart Protein Drink box from the ground up, as well as the Garcinia supplement line. A primary focus was developing precise, bilingual packaging (English and French) specifically tailored for the Canadian market. The design strategy successfully balanced the brand's clean aesthetic with the complex regulatory and nutritional information required for bilingual compliance.
